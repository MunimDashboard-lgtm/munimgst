How the amount is calculated

Price = every rule below, added together. For a metered rule only the quantity above the included amount is charged. A band rule charges the fee of the selected band. A yes/no rule charges its fee only when ticked. This table always shows the current live rules.

RuleIncluded freeRateBilling
  1. Recurring per year = sum of all yearly amounts.
  2. One-time = sum of all one-time amounts.
  3. First-year total = (recurring + one-time) + GST (). From year 2 = recurring + GST.
  4. B2B invoices are part of the total invoice count, so a B2B invoice pays both the general invoice rate and its B2B rate. Included amounts apply per account, not per GSTIN.

Assumptions to review

  • Turnover bands, invoice, B2B, e-way, e-invoice, consultancy and end-to-end integration rates were assumptions; no competitor publishes them.
  • Munim's Billing & Accounting plans include e-way bills and e-invoices today at no extra charge. Those fees are meant for the GST / enterprise offering.
  • Munim's GSTIN pricing (about ₹120–150 per GSTIN) is built for CAs; competitors charge ₹5,250–25,000 for a single-GSTIN business.
  • Competitor figures are floors or list prices from vendor pages and third-party listings and may be out of date.
